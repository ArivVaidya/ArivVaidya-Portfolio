Research

See research →

At the Khurana Lab (Harvard Medical School & Brigham and Women’s Hospital), I worked on neurodegeneration projects. I helped curate and analyze data from the Harvard Biomarker Study, a longitudinal Parkinson’s cohort. We identified subtypes of Parkinson’s by progression rate and used machine learning to predict these subtypes from baseline features. I also assisted with lab assays and shadowed neurology cases.

Report

A write-up of the approach, methods, and results used to analyze and predict patient outcomes. Presented at the World Pre-Health Conference and the Massachusetts State Science & Engineering Fair.
