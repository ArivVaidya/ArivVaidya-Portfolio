Mediar Therapeutics — RNA-seq Dashboard

Mediar Therapeutics is a biotech developing anti-fibrotic therapies. I built a web app (R/flexdashboard & Shiny) that lets scientists explore and visualize RNA-seq data: filter genes, compare conditions, view volcano/heatmap/boxplot outputs, and export figures for slide-ready use. (The demo ships with dummy data; you can also import your own.)

Tip To get started inside the app, click the Data Analysis tab.
  • Upload or select datasets (example uses dummy data).
  • Interactive differential expression tables with search and significance cutoffs.
  • Quick plots (volcano, heatmap, boxplot) with one-click export.
