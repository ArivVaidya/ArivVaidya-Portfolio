A look at what I do outside academics — music, athletics, service, and leadership.

Music

  • 2023–2025: Three-time BISB Best Musician of the Year

Tennis

As captain of the varsity team, I organized after-school practices and set match strategies for singles and doubles. We went undefeated and won our league championship. Earlier, I trained at Sportsmen’s Tennis & Enrichment Center and competed with a UTR of 5.55.

Community Service

Alexander Twilight Academy

ATA supports high-potential students from under-resourced backgrounds. As a Math Teaching Assistant, I created practice materials, taught lessons, and ran small-group breakouts with 6th graders to work through problems step by step.

Volley Against Violence

A program that connects local youth and police through tennis. I assisted coaches, set up courts and equipment, led drills, and spent time talking with students during activities.

Current Events Club — Founder

I set up a program for middle and high school students to write and debate contemporary issues—politics, environment, technology, and economics. We’ve published 50+ articles in the school paper. This year I’m Head of the Bulldog Inquirer, leading publication and supporting the editorial team on a site with 200+ articles.

Science & MIT Ambassador

I helped run activities at the Cambridge Science Festival and led hands-on demos for younger students. I also contributed to MIT outreach content and collaborated with MIT PhD students on testing camera kits for tracking the aurora borealis.

Peer Mentor

Support younger students as they transition into high school—planning homework, sharing study tips and resources, and checking in on progress.

Admissions Representative

Represent BISB during open houses and tours. Welcome visiting families, answer questions, and help new students get oriented on their first day.
